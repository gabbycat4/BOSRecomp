BLADES OF STEEL RECOMP - NOTES
==============================

     (128 KB PRG, CHR RAM, mapper 2 = UxROM, vertical mirroring)

Status (2026-11-13)
- Phases 0-2 done; route.txt reaches the opening faceoff (~frame 2400).
- route_play.txt = route.txt + ~4 minutes of random play after the faceoff,
  to harvest gameplay code and record logs.
- src/bos_extras.c v1: edge crop, no sprite flicker, smooth camera,
  120/180/240 pictures per second, --scroll-log / --oam-log.

Measurements (fill in from scroll.log / oam.log / Mesen)
- 100.0% native over route_play.txt (18500 frames) - done.
- Camera: X moves EVERY frame (60 Hz) when it moves, steps 1-7 px
  (often a steady 2, 4 or 5 px/frame). Y moves a little (+-1/2) sometimes.
  Scroll spans 512 (two nametables).
- No HUD split: the whole screen (rows 0-239) is one scrolling playfield.
- Busiest line: up to 15 sprites (above 8 = the NES's flicker).
- OAM order: the game rotates its list every frame (same sprites,
  different slots), so ties must not be broken by slot number.
- WHITE FLASH: players switch from their team palette (0 or 1) to
  palette 3 for 2 frames out of every 6 (10 Hz). FlashSoften handles it.
- Players move EVERY frame (60 Hz), mostly 1-3 px, up to ~5.
- Player glide (CharGlide) tested offline on oam.log: about 90% of moving
  sprite-frames glide; the rest (players bunched up, pose changes the
  matching can't follow) are drawn where the game put them.
  Low lag: a player's step changes 0.27 px on average from frame to frame.

Bugs seen while playing (where / what):

Camera on long shots (scroll.log, frames 4101-4160 and others): the game's
camera snaps from +2 to -7 px/frame at once, skips frames (-7 0 -7 0) and
stops dead. SmoothCamera now eases it: accel 1.6 (Quick) / 1.0 (Gentle)
px/frame per frame, brakes onto the real camera without passing it, and
draws the trailing columns from the nametables. Offline on scroll.log:
trails the game's camera by at most ~32 px; a few small bumps where the
game's camera turns back toward it.

Pacing (2026-11-13): NES emulation takes ~7.5-8.5 ms a frame on the Ryzen
2700, so the engine now runs it on a second thread (present_from_snapshot)
and holds each frame's picture to an even slot. 180 is flat (4.4-7.5 ms);
240 does not fit (and is 240.4, above the monitor). Rewind's snapshot every
4 frames slowed the next frame to ~21 ms after a goal: rewind now starts
off (NESRECOMP_REWIND_DEFAULT=0 in CMakeLists.txt).

Defaults (what a fresh config.ini gets): Steady glow + Gold highlight,
Softest camera, no sprite flicker, lower-on-screen-in-front overlaps,
Player glide Smooth, 60 pictures a second (players with fast monitors
pick 120/180), edge crop 8, rewind off.
